🎨 How to Play DoodleClash

1. When it's your turn to Draw:

Choose 1 of 3 secret words. Use the brush, pencil, shapes, and paint bucket to sketch it before time expires! The faster people guess, the more points you earn.

2. When you are Guessing:

Watch the canvas live! Type your guesses into the chat box. Guess quickly for maximum speed bonus points and multiplier streaks.

3. Progressive Hints:

As the clock ticks down, letter blanks _ _ _ _ will gradually reveal letters.

4. Solo / AI Bot Support:

You can add AI Bots anytime to fill matches or practice solo!
